Your adventures.
Your data. Your Mac.

Meet RaceBox Connect, a native Mac companion for your RaceBox Mini S. Bring your recordings home, watch live telemetry, and take a closer look at every turn.

Bluetooth to your RaceBox. Straight to your Mac.

Less friction. More exploring.

Everything you need.
None of the cloud.

No account. No subscription. No uploading your recordings.
Just a direct Bluetooth connection and files you actually own.

↓

Bring every session home

Download stored recordings as JSON or CSV. Sessions are organized by date, and recordings you've already downloaded are skipped.

⌁

Catch it live

Stream telemetry straight to disk. Live capture waits for a valid 3D fix and pauses if the fix is lost.

↗

Relive every turn

Explore tracks on Apple Maps, switch to satellite view, and select a moment in the speed and G-force charts to find it on the map.

◎

A little signal check

See speed, GNSS accuracy, satellites used, battery, motion, and memory status in one friendly dashboard.

▣

Record. Disconnect. Go.

Start and stop standalone recording on your Mini S. Recording in device memory keeps going after you disconnect.

⚙

Make yourself at home

Choose your download folder, JSON or CSV, metric or imperial units, and optional automatic connection. Built with SwiftUI and CoreBluetooth.

Your recording files stay local. Apple Maps requires an internet connection to load map tiles.

Feels right at home

A little look around.

A real Mac app, from the first connection to the last corner.
Select any screenshot for a closer look. Recordings use sample data; device identifiers are redacted.

Ready when you are

Bring your data home.

Download the DMG, drag RaceBox Connect to Applications, and allow Bluetooth access. Power on your Mini S and close any other apps connected to it. Your next adventure awaits.
